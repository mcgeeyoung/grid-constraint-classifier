D: per hour. Here, 12 events. Atomic but noisy.

A

Contiguous dispatch block

Stressed+extreme hours adjacent = one event. Matches how program operators think ("morning event, afternoon event"). Mixes optional and mandatory hours — report with sub-breakdown.

B

Contiguous extreme-only block

Mandatory hours only, gaps close an event. Aligns with emergency DR conventions. Stressed-only activity is called out separately.

C

DA day

One row per operating day with any dispatch. Simple. Loses intra-day structure; harder to reason about multi-event days.

D

Per hour

Every non-zero program-signal hour = event. Noisiest. The history table would be huge, the "average per event" reduces to hourly averaging.
