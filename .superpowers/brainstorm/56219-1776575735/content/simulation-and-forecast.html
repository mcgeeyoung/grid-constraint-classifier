Mocked telemetry "story" + next-day forecast source

Two small decisions that shape how the demo feels.

Telemetry story

Realized kW per device per hour is mocked. What character should the mock have so the dashboard tells a story, not just flat averages?

1

Uniform, slight noise

All devices 85–92%, ±5% per hour. Clean. Every zone looks the same → dashboard doesn't tell a program-design story.

2

Per-device characteristic

Each device has a baseline perf (seeded). TYSONS 90%, BRADDOCK 78%, BLMNTDOM 94%, etc. Ranked "top / bottom performers" makes sense.

3

Zone × duration shape (recommended)

Per-device baseline (as in 2) plus intra-event decay (performance drops hour 3+ on long events) plus zone-level color (Loudoun exurban: steady residential thermostats; Fairfax 230 kV: growing data centers, more noise; Alexandria: small sample). Makes the demo's charts speak.

Expected next-day dispatch source

"Today's forecast" and "tomorrow's forecast" need a data source. PJM DA clears ~4 pm day-ahead — so tomorrow's DA may or may not exist when a user loads the page.

A

PJM DA for tomorrow when available, else today (recommended)

If DA for tomorrow is ingested (after ~4 pm EPT), show it. Otherwise show today's DA as the "tomorrow likely looks like this" proxy. Label clearly. No ML.

B

Rolling-window average

"Tomorrow will look like the last 7 weekday DA days." Simple statistical forecast. Works even before DA clears. Less credible to a program operator ("why not use actual DA?").

C

Light ML

A small regression over hour-of-week + weather. Better forecast, but scope creep. Not worth it for the demo.
